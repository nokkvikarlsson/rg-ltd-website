About

Renown Global Limited, based in Nairobi, Kenya, gives brands a direct line to engaged customers. Players earn real rewards — airtime, data, vouchers — by playing well inside our mobile games, and sponsor brands get their name in front of that audience with a full delivery and audit trail proving exactly who was reached. The game is the front door; the audience and the reach are what we sell.
